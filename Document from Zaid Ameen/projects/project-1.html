Client Onboarding Automation System

Role: No-Code Builder Tools: Zapier · Airtable · Google Workspace Type: Freelance Project
DEMO HERO IMAGE — swap me

The Problem

A service business was onboarding new clients manually — copying form responses into spreadsheets, sending welcome emails one by one, and forgetting follow-ups. Every new client meant an hour of repetitive admin, and details slipped through the cracks.

The Solution

I designed and built a fully automated onboarding pipeline: the moment a client submits a form, the system creates their record, sends a personalized welcome email, assigns internal tasks, and logs everything in a central Airtable base.

  • Form submission triggers a multi-step Zapier workflow
  • Client record auto-created in Airtable with tags & pipeline stage
  • Personalized welcome email + intro packet sent via Gmail
  • Slack notification to the team with next-action checklist
  • Follow-up reminders scheduled automatically

The Impact

Onboarding time dropped from ~60 minutes of manual work to under 2 minutes of review. Nothing gets forgotten, every client gets the same polished first impression, and the team reclaimed hours every week.
