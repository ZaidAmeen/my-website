Sales Order Tracker Dashboard

Role: Builder & Designer Tools: Notion · Google Sheets · Looker Studio Type: Freelance Project
DEMO HERO IMAGE — swap me

The Problem

A small business tracked orders across WhatsApp messages, paper notes and a spreadsheet nobody updated. Orders got delayed, invoices were missed, and nobody had a clear picture of what was pending.

The Solution

I built a single source of truth: a clean Notion database for order entry, synced with Google Sheets, feeding a live Looker Studio dashboard the owner checks every morning with coffee.

  • Order intake form feeding a structured Notion database
  • Auto-calculated statuses: New → In Progress → Dispatched → Paid
  • Invoice tracking with overdue highlights
  • Client follow-up reminders tied to order age
  • Live dashboard: revenue, pending orders, overdue invoices

The Impact

Missed invoices dropped to zero in the first month. The owner went from chasing information to seeing it at a glance — and follow-ups now happen on time, every time.
